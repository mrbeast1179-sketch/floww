## 5. Evidence Summary

1. **Empirical win-rate distribution from DVT v2**: the calibration band (0.55-0.70) contains the **largest cohort** of strategy records — see Section 2 above. This is what justifies anchoring Kelly at p=0.55 rather than the (lower) breakeven p.
2. **Naive fixed-2% under-bets** when win-rate approaches 0.55+: see Section 4. Half-Kelly at p=0.55, b=1.65 risks ~6.8× more dollars per trade than the naive rule, which is appropriate given the >50% win-rate profile.
3. **Delta-adjusted sizing** (Section 6 — see `backend/domain/position_sizing.py`) is the *position-rounding* counterpart: it converts the per-trade dollar budget into the *integer number of contracts* bounded by the budget under the actual option delta. Kelly says *how much %*, delta-adjusted sizing says *how many contracts*.

## 6. Sample-Size Caveat

Total records analysed: **18**. The following bucket(s) have fewer than 10 records and the empirical Kelly fractions in Section 2 should be read as directional estimates only: `0.40-0.55 (approaching edge)`, `0.55-0.70 (calibration band)`, `0.70-1.00 (strong edge)`. The calibration anchor numbers in Section 3 are **theoretical**, not empirical, and are anchored at the historical `p=0.55, b=1.65` run-rate.
