## KEY FEATURES FROM SKYLIT ACADEMY

### Reading Heatseeker Maps
1. **King Node**: Largest absolute exposure value. Center of structural gravity. One per session.
2. **Floors**: Large nodes below spot. Dealers buy on approach. Strongest = largest exposure below spot.
3. **Ceilings**: Large nodes above spot. Dealers sell on approach. Strongest = largest exposure above spot.
4. **Gatekeeper Nodes**: Sit between spot and King Node. Checkpoints that determine if price reaches target.
5. **Air Pockets**: Low-resistance pathways between nodes. Price accelerates through these.

### Gamma Regime Awareness
- **Positive Gamma = Range Days**: Dealers dampen volatility, mean-reversion plays
- **Negative Gamma = Trend Days**: Dealers amplify moves, momentum trades
- **Mixed Signals = Whipsaw Days**: Conflicting signs, reduce size or sit out

### Heatseeker Patterns
1. **Rug**: Positive ceiling above, negative below → rejection with pro-cyclical acceleration down
2. **Reverse Rug**: Positive floor below, negative above → support holds, bounce runs
3. **Pika Cloud**: Dense positive cluster → inefficient transit zone
4. **Beach Ball**: Spot stretched past king node → overshoot/reversion setup
5. **Whipsaw**: Conflicting signs near spot → fade extremes only
6. **Rainbow Road**: No dominant structure, chaos → sit out

### The Execution Doctrine
- **Tap Probability Decay**: 80% → 66% → 33% → 10% (fresh → tested → delivered → decaying)
- **Deflection Zones**: Enter at direct node taps
- **3:1 Minimum R:R**: Risk management rule
- **Never Trade the Midpoint**: Between nodes is no-man's land

### Node Lifecycle
- **Fresh**: Never tested, strongest reactions, growing intent
- **Tested**: Tapped once, weakening
- **Delivered**: Tapped twice, significantly weakened
- **Decaying**: Tapped 3+ times, fading protection
- **Real Nodes vs Hedge Nodes**: Real = growing intent, Hedge = fading protection

### Rolling Floors and Ceilings
- Floors rolling higher = dealers repositioning bullish
- Ceilings rolling lower = dealers repositioning bearish
- Early evidence of trend formation

### Air Pockets, Liquidity Vacuums, Velocity Mode
- **Air Pockets**: Low-resistance pathways between nodes
- **Liquidity Vacuums**: Extended runways where price accelerates
- **Velocity Mode**: Shows dealer urgency in real-time

### Trinity Mode
- Cross-index alignment across SPX, SPY, QQQ
- Full alignment = high probability setup
- Divergence = caution
- Markets move as a system

### The Greeks (Complete Library)

- **Vega**: Volatility sensitivity, governs vol regime changes

- **Charm**: Delta's sensitivity to time, drives afternoon drift
- **Vomma**: Vega's sensitivity to volatility, non-linear repricing
